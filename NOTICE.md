# Project notices

## Project development

Project development: **Paul of UK Computing Limited**, **Jon of JMC Electronics** and **AI assistance through Codex**. It develops Paul's horizontal straddle-PCB concept and supported individual-pin construction. Revision 0.5 adds the shorter mounting-ear PCB, solid base, upward metal fasteners and keyed clip-on cap.

No third-party logo has been recreated or added by assumption.

## Reference information

Nominal motherboard connector dimensions are taken from iComp's documented DB23 replacement parts, identified by iComp as matching the original Commodore/Foxconn parts. Links and the scope of that information are in [docs/SOURCES.md](docs/SOURCES.md).

Manufacturer drawings establish nominal geometry for the documented replacement parts. They do not identify the mechanical dimensions of an arbitrary solder-cup cable connector. Reference connector shapes in assembly views are illustrative until a purchased part has been measured.

Commodore's A4000 User's Guide is referenced for interface and grounding information. Product names remain the property of their respective owners. This replacement project is not represented as a product endorsed by those owners.

Third-party source documents are linked for reference rather than republished as project-owned work. Fonts and software used to create previews do not imply a licence for their manufacturers' hardware designs.

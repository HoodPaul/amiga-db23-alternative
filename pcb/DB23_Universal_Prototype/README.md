# Universal DB23 PCB — experimental prototype

**USE THE UNIVERSAL PCB AT YOUR OWN RISK.** It has not been physically fitted or functionally tested on an Amiga. CAD checks and a PCB order do not establish compatibility or safe operation.

This separate board snapshot is based on PCB revision 0.5. It is intended for either a male RGB video solder-cup connector or a female external-floppy solder-cup connector, with the correct connector orientation. Both faces carry VIDEO 1 and FLOPPY 1 markers at their respective ends; other pin numbers are removed and all signal through-hole pads are round. The centre of each pin-1 label aligns with its rear-row through-hole.

Keep the same physical PCB orientation for either connector: 12-contact cups on the top face, 11-contact cups on the bottom, connector facing outwards. Confirm this against your actual connector. Bottom silkscreen is mirrored for reading from below. Internal pad/net numbers retain the VIDEO reference numbering; they are not floppy connector numbers. No universal schematic is supplied.

## Checks and limitations

KiCad 10.0.7 loaded the board and ran native DRC: zero errors and zero unconnected items; four warnings concern the absent DB23_Local footprint library. Embedded footprints are included in the PCB. No geometry or silkscreen violations were reported. Schematic parity and ERC were not run.

[integrity.json](integrity.json) confirms the unchanged 26 tracks and outline, 50 pads and only the intended square-to-round pad change. [connection-check.json](connection-check.json) verifies all 23 independent J1-to-J2 signal nets. [Native DRC report](drc.json) is retained. Exports used kicad-cli pcb export gerbers, drill and svg; DRC used kicad-cli pcb drc --format json. Local commands logged registry/configuration warnings, but output files and the DRC report were produced.

Check actual connector numbering, all 23 end-to-end connections, isolation between signals, separate SHIELD wiring, mechanical clearances and mounting insulation before powering hardware. Retain the deliberate connector-shell and motherboard shell-ground bonds described in the main assembly instructions.

## Selected 11.5 mm pins

Use 23 equal-length pins per adapter, untrimmed after removing the plastic carrier. The nominal 0.64 mm square section remains a design assumption to confirm on arrival.

With the existing jig lower-tip stop at Z−3.20, 11.50 mm pins end at Z8.30: **1.55 mm above the nominal PCB top at Z6.75**, leaving **2.70 mm to the lid roof underside at Z11.00**. These are nominal arithmetic clearances, not a physical or regenerated CAD pass. Check solder joints, connector cups and actual lid fit. Main CAD source still models 10.70 mm pins.

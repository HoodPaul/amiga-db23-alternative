DB23 HOLDER v0.3: MECHANICAL FIT PROTOTYPE

The mating connector is a genuine DB23 solder-cup part. Its assumed rear body
and solder-cup dimensions have NOT been measured. This part is an actual
printable fit trial, not a guarantee of compatibility with a bought connector.

REBUILD
From the project root: python3 source/build_mechanical.py
The builder reads source/design_parameters.json, emits the editable OpenSCAD
file, compiles each STL using native OpenSCAD, checks manifold edges and mesh
connectivity, and exports an analytic single-solid STEP holder with CadQuery.
The 23 uninterrupted pin bores are also checked by intersecting the STEP solid
with a test cylinder inside every bore. OpenSCAD is the canonical STL route.

PRINTED PARTS
1 x DB23_Holder_v0.3.stl
1 x DB23_Hole_Fit_Coupon_v0.3.stl, before committing to a pin-guide diameter
2 x DB23_Nut_Keeper_v0.3.stl, optional; screw stems retain nuts once installed
1 x DB23_Anchor_Shims_v0.3.stl, containing TWO 0.60 mm shims
Alternatively print DB23_Anchor_Shim_Single_v0.3.stl twice.

The main holder has one connected structural mesh. The shim pair deliberately
contains two separate printable components. The coupon contains the actual
23-hole staggered grid at the selected nominal diameter and eight labelled
holes from 0.75 to 1.10 mm at 0.05 mm increments.

PRINT ORIENTATION
Place the broad planar bottom of the holder on the bed. The exported holder
retains assembly Z coordinates, with its bottom at Z=0.60, so use the slicer's
drop-to-bed function. The nut keeper is already exported cap-face down, tongue
up. Coupon and shims already sit at Z=0. Small jackpost horizontal bores and
the ends of nut pockets may need local support or subsequent drilling,
depending on printer, material and settings. Clear all support/debris.

Do not assume that the nominal STL hole diameter equals a printed hole. Select
the guide diameter after printing the coupon, or carefully finish the bores
with an appropriate drill/reamer. A 0.64 mm square pin has a 0.9051 mm diagonal.
A finished 0.80 mm round guide is therefore not a clearance fit for that pin.
The selected nominal 0.92 mm geometry has very little theoretical clearance;
printer compensation or a larger coupon-selected diameter may be required.

Avoid installing a close-fitting printed pin guide while applying sustained
soldering heat. Use a removable alignment jig where practical, and check that
all pins remain straight and unstressed afterwards. Actual printed material,
temperature capability and creep need assessment in the target machine.

ASSEMBLY COORDINATES (mm)
X is across the port, Y points into the machine, Z is above motherboard top.
Main holder: leave STL at its exported coordinates.
Anchor shim centres: X=+/-22.135, Y=9.50, bottom Z=0.00, top Z=0.60.
The pair STL has centres X=+/-5.00 at Y=0; separate/position the two components.
The single-shim STL is centred on X=0,Y=0 and needs only a translation.
Keeper STL: rotate 180 degrees about Y, then translate X=+/-22.135,
Y=0, Z=12.20. Its cap then lies from Z=11.00 to 12.20, with its tongue
projecting down to Z=9.80. Do not apply the slicer's print orientation to the
assembly render. All measurements here refer to the v0.3 defaults.

FASTENERS AND CLEARANCES
Cable-facing jackpost sockets must use the original-compatible 4-40 UNC thread.
The rear stem and captive nut must match each other. Current prototype pockets
assume an M3-sized nut, 5.50 mm across flats and 2.40 mm thick, plus 0.20 mm
total pocket clearance. This does not mean any random D-sub jackpost is M3.

The flange FRONT reference plane is Y=0; its assumed metal thickness occupies
Y=0..0.50. The holder front face is Y=0.65. This preserves 0.15 mm assembly
clearance behind the assumed flange; final clamping fit needs physical checks.
The reference nut pocket occupies Y=1.70..4.30. Keep the jackpost rear stem end
at Y<=5.50, preferably around Y=4.80, measured from the flange reference datum.
Select or trim the stem only after measuring the actual flange and hardware.
An 8 mm rear stem can collide with the motherboard anchor bolt head.

Anchor bosses have their top at Z=2.40. Use anchor bolt heads AND any washers
whose combined height is <=3.00 mm and outer diameter <=6.00 mm. This places
the head top at Z<=5.40, below the assumed PCB underside at Z=5.75. A taller
head/washer stack can strike the PCB. No 8 mm jackpost is specified as a
drop-in part. Allow clearance under the motherboard for retention hardware.

Fit the supplied 0.60 mm shims under the two mounting bosses before tightening.
They span the intentional 0.60 mm frame-to-motherboard gap. Without the shims,
tightening the anchors would pull the frame down and strain the signal pins.
The frame clearance holes are 3.30 mm; do not enlarge the original motherboard
holes to match them. Verify the original holes and select suitable hardware.
The PCB front corners must have the matching 2.00 x 1.50 mm notches present in
the shared PCB outline, to clear the two nut towers. A plain 40 x 12 mm
rectangle collides with those towers. Lateral stops have 0.25 mm clearance
outside each nominal PCB side edge; adjust for the actual printed fit.

Captive nuts alone do not anchor the assembly to the motherboard. The two
positive retention fasteners or a suitable chassis attachment must take cable
mating loads. Plastic locating pegs are not a substitute for that retention.
Provide a separate metal shell-ground bond to the original shield-ground
points. Plastic holders and shims do not provide electrical grounding.

LOCAL SHAPE PARAMETERS
The generated SCAD exposes every shared and local parameter. Additional local
shape choices in source/build_mechanical.py include rail width/height, nut
tower width/depth/height, rear ledge depth, shield access slots, 2.40 mm anchor
boss height and keeper fit clearance. These are design choices, not sourced
Commodore or solder-cup connector dimensions. The shield pad positions and
23-hole motherboard grid come from the shared parameter file.

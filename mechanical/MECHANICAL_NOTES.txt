DB23 REPLACEMENT v0.5: MECHANICAL FIT PROTOTYPE

The genuine solder-cup DB23 and bought hardware are not measured samples.
Print fit, cap retention, cable insertion strength and soldering heat tolerance
remain untested. Black is the intended printed appearance, not a material spec.

FILES AND REBUILD
From project root: python3 source/build_mechanical.py
The builder reads source/design_parameters.json. One constructive geometry
model generates editable OpenSCAD, native OpenSCAD STL and analytic STEP.
Run source/verify_mechanical.py for the separate saved-file geometry audit.
For a base/cap-only edit, --parts holder cap re-exports those STL files while
topology-checking the existing unchanged trial-part STLs. A full build remains
the default and is needed if those trial parts or their parameters change.

Print one each:
 DB23_Holder_v0.5.stl          solid lower base, in assembly coordinates
 DB23_Cap_v0.5.stl             keyed cosmetic cap, exported roof-down
 DB23_Pin_Height_Jig_v0.5.stl   loading only, not installed
 DB23_Hole_Fit_Coupon_v0.5.stl  pin-hole trials before selecting bore diameter
 DB23_Clip_Fit_Base_v0.5.stl    small representative latch-fit trial
 DB23_Clip_Fit_Cap_v0.5.stl     mating clip-fit trial
STEP files for base and cap retain assembly coordinates. Jig STEP, like its
STL, is on the print bed. Clip-fit parts are only fit aids, not load-rated parts.
No separate mounting shims and no printed expanding motherboard pegs are used.

Flange front Y0; illustrative flange metal Y0..0.50, base front Y0.65.
PCB underside Z5.75, thickness1.00; nominal mating centreline Z6.25.
Base width52.00, rearY13.00; PCB overall width51.20, rearY13.00.
Cap width52.00, rearY13.20, roof undersideZ11.00, roof topZ12.20.
The two forward end faces are now continuous square planes at X+/-25.60
from Y0.65 through Y5.10. The marked rear corners have been filled from X25.00
to X25.60; the former front X26.00 surface is trimmed to the same plane so
there is no re-entrant step through the end wall. Matching cap corners follow
that plane. The unchanged nut pockets leave nominal0.615mm outer walls and
0.80mm rear webs. These dimensions are not a measured strength rating.
The local X25.00 neighbour-lug relief now starts BEHIND the end towers at
Y5.10 and ends at Y7.50. Overall aft width remains52.00. Actual target revision,
neighbouring metal lug/solder fillet and connector housing still need a fit
check; no new height-dependent clearance assumption is used.
The assumed shell/cup envelope, base seat and flange clamping need a real
bearing check. Fit a measured bearing spacer if needed; do not strain cups or
signal tails to close an assumed gap.

BASE AND SUPPORTS
Only the two integrated annular mounting feet reach motherboard Z0. The rest
of the main body remains Z0.60 above the motherboard. The former loose0.60
shims have been integrated into these feet without changing the PCB height.
Mounting axes remain X+/-22.135,Y9.50; base and new PCB holes are diameter3.30.
The referenced motherboard holes remain nominal3.20: DO NOT enlarge or drill
the original motherboard to match printed clearances.

The23-pin guide uses nominal0.92 bores for assumed0.64 square pins. Their
corner diagonal is0.9051. A finished0.80 round bore cannot clear them. Print
the hole coupon first and establish a snug retaining fit with actual pins.
The guide top isZ4.50, leaving1.25 below the PCB for fillets/heat separation.
Rear material is continuous. Two shallow relief pockets below J4 shell-wire
holes at X+/-18,Y7.00 have floorsZ4.50 and3.90mm of material beneath them.
Pre-solder any underside joints before lowering the joined PCB/DB23.

UPWARD MOUNTING SCREWS
Default is M3x12, measured under the head. Assumed head diameter7.00 and
height1.00 are based on the selected ultra-thin style and require measurement.
A mandatory insulating washer beneath the motherboard is provisionally0.50
thick, OD8.00, ID3.20. Reference CAD shows that a bare7mm head can overlap
unrelated copper. The insulating washer's actual coverage and compression,
underside traces, solder joints and chassis clearance must be verified.
There is NO top washer in the default stack. The nut bears directly on the
supported6.50mm exposed SHIELD land of the adapter PCB. Use tested light
clamping only; print creep and long-term fastener retention remain untested.
An optional rigid compression stop needs a reviewed bore variant and an
actual measured length, nominal5.75. It is not required by this fit prototype.

Assuming a1.60mm motherboard,0.50mm underside insulator,5.75mm base height,
1.00mm adapter and2.40mm nut gives11.25mm to the top of the nut. M3x12 leaves
0.75mm nominal protrusion. TipZ9.90, nut topZ9.15, roof clearance1.10mm.
An optional top washer consumes protrusion. M3x14 andx16 need trimming or a
revised cap; they are NOT stated to fit the default cover. Verify actual nut
engagement, bolt length and underboard clearance before assembly.

SHELL GROUNDING AND WIRE CAVITY
The insulated underside screw heads cannot be relied on for shell continuity.
Provide a deliberate wire bond from an original motherboard shell-ground pad
to J4, and from J4/SHIELD to the DB23 metal shell. Neither captive nuts nor
plastic carry this electrical bond. Keep SHIELD separate from signal grounds.
The RIGHT integrated foot contains a radial wire/solder relief: X24.00 to
the outside edge, Y9.50+/-0.60, Z0..1.00. Pre-tack the ground lead to the actual
shell-pad annulus, route its insulated section outwards and then up to J4.
Check real joint/insulation fit so the foot sits flat and does not crush the
wire. The rest of the annular foot remains the bearing surface. Do not route
bare wire across the23-pin signal field or obstruct the cap latches.

JACKPOST NUTS AND STEMS
Cable-facing sockets require original-compatible4-40UNC. The rear stem and
captive nut must match; prototype defaults are M3. The assumed nut is5.50AF,
2.40thick, with0.20total pocket allowance. Its axis isZ6.25,Y3.00.
The printed horizontal passage is diameter3.40 and extends toY6.20. The
conservative permitted rear stem endpoint isY<=6.00 from the flange front,
preferably around4.80. This limit includes the TOP motherboard mounting nuts,
not merely the vertical bolt shanks. A longer stem may hit them. Select or
trim actual hardware only after measurement; no claim that every jackpost
length fits is made.

CAP AND LATCH FIT
The cap is cosmetic and retains the jackpost nuts; the solid base and metal
through-bolts provide the intended main cable-load attachment. Nut-pocket
keys project toZ9.90. Two inboard latches behind the front towers have nominal
0.60mm beam thickness,1.40mm width and0.20mm engagement. Nominal insertion
needs about0.20mm rearward movement, with additional fit allowance. This is a
geometric estimate, not an elastic strength or fatigue result.
Print BOTH clip-fit trial pieces first, with the intended material, nozzle
and settings. Remove burrs/support and verify gentle assembly, retention and
removal before printing/forcing the full cap. Adjust shared parameters for
fit. Side/rear skirt reliefs deliberately clear the upper mounting nuts.
Fit the cap LAST, after mounting, soldering and continuity inspection.

PRINT ORIENTATION AND SUPPORTS
Base STL retains feetZ0, bodyZ0.60. Its lower face is NOT fully planar: provide
local removable support beneath the main body/guide where needed. Do not
assume an untested wide bridge over the0.60 gap will print accurately. Keep
supports clear of small bores and remove all debris before pin loading.
The cap STL is already roof-down: the flat outer roof rests on the bed and
keys/latches build upwards. Local hook ramps need actual print trials.
The coupon, jig and both clip-fit trial parts are already on the print bed.

Assembly transform for cap STL: rotate180degrees about X, then translate
Z+12.20. This self-inverse transform restores the assembly-coordinate cap.
For jig STL/STEP: translateZ-6.20 to restore its loading position.
Do not apply slicer drop-to-bed to assembly renders of the base or cap STEP.

PIN HEIGHT JIG
The jig supports the integrated mounting feet atZ0 with loose2.90mm locating
pegs in the3.30mm bores. Its common flat floor stops all23 tips atZ-3.20.
Equal10.70mm pin lengths then place the upper ends atZ7.50. Equal LOWER
projection depends on clean seating and flat stops; equal TOP heights also
require equal cut lengths. Verify guide grip retains the pins on transfer.
Remove the jig before lowering/soldering the PCB, and never use the Amiga
motherboard as the pin-forming or soldering fixture.

BUILD ORDER
1. Solder the DB23 and PCB together; prepare covered underside shell joints.
2. Load23pins into base on height jig. Remove jig, lower joinedPCB/DB23,
   seat it without force, and solder pin tops using a tested heat strategy.
3. Fit jackpost nuts/hardware, check flange bearing and finite stem clearance.
4. Pre-tack motherboard shell-ground wire and route through the foot cavity.
   Install adapter; insert insulated-head screws upward and fit top nuts.
   Secure flat seating, solder motherboard tails and complete shell bonds.
5. Inspect23independent circuits and the separate shell path; fit cap last.

All dimensional, electrical and mechanical checks remain scoped to this fit
prototype. Actual print strength, assembly forces, soldering heat performance,
case fit, hardware contact and Amiga operation are not validated.

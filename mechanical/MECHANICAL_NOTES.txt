AMIGA DB23: MECHANICAL REVISION 0.6 / PCB REVISION 0.5
FIT PROTOTYPE - revision 0.6 has not yet been physically printed or tested.
Paul's revision 0.5 print showed delicate lid projections and a nut within the
printed footprint. The full PCB, nut and screw stack remains unmeasured.

Superseded mechanical files and the obsolete clip-fit pair are removed.

COORDINATES AND PRINT ORIENTATION (mm)
X across the port, Y inwards from flange front Y0, Z above motherboard top Z0.
Base: broad flat underside at Z0, maximum width 52, rear Y13.
PCB: underside Z5.75, thickness 1.00, mating centreline Z6.25.
Cover: maximum width 52, rear Y13.20, roof Z11.00 to Z12.60.
Restore cap STL to assembly by rotating 180 degrees around X, then Z+12.60.
Restore jig STL/STEP to loading position by translating Z-6.20.
No separate mounting shims or projecting feet. Keep the original motherboard
holes unchanged; no drilling or countersinking of the motherboard is specified.

SIMPLIFIED OPTIONAL COVER
Roof thickness 1.60. Short side/rear rim thickness 1.20, bottom Z10.30.
Front skirt bottom Z10.20. Two broad locating tabs extend down to Z9.90.
Four thin corner projections and both hooks are removed. The corresponding
base catches are filled solid. This is not a snap-fit design.
Dry-fit the cover with the PCB and nuts installed. After final inspection,
tiny superglue spots on the tower tops may hold it. Keep adhesive away from
contacts, pockets and threads. A glued cover may be damaged during removal.
Fitted jackposts must retain their nuts with the cover absent. The cover is
cosmetic; the solid base and through-bolts provide the intended cable load path.

PIN GUIDES AND COUPON
Each of the 23 guides has a 45-degree funnel, depth 0.40 from Z4.10 to Z4.50,
mouth diameter 1.72 and straight retaining bore diameter 0.92 below it.
This equals two layers at 0.20 layer height. The coupon uses the same depth
on its trial bores and 23-hole grid. Test actual printer and pin tolerances.
Assumed 0.64 square pins have diagonal 0.9051; a finished 0.80 bore is too small.
A hand pin vice can clear a trial hole; confirm snug retention afterwards.
Hot pressing is unvalidated and may distort the guide or pin alignment.
Remove all debris before loading pins. Guide top remains 1.25 below the PCB.
Equal 10.70 pins run from Z-3.20 to Z7.50. The jig supports the base at Z0 and
sets a common lower stop at Z-3.20. Remove it and recheck grip before lowering
the joined PCB/DB23. The motherboard is not a forming or pin-loading fixture.

CORNERS AND HARDWARE
Forward tower outer faces remain X+/-25.60 over Y0.65 to Y5.10.
Neighbour relief remains X+/-25.00 from Y5.10 to Y7.50, behind the towers.
Actual neighbouring metal, solder fillets and housing need a clearance check.
Mounting axes X+/-22.135, Y9.50. Base and adapter bores diameter 3.30.
Default: M3 x 12 screws upwards from beneath motherboard, diameter 7 x 1 heads,
diameter 8 x 0.50 insulating washers below heads, top nuts 5.50 AF x 2.40
on the supported PCB mounting lands. No default top washers.
Assumed motherboard 1.60 + underside washer 0.50 + base height 5.75 + PCB 1.00
+ nut 2.40 gives 11.25 under-head stack. Tip Z9.90; nut top Z9.15;
nominal protrusion 0.75; roof underside clearance above tip 1.10.
Head bottom Z-3.10: check underside copper and chassis clearance. Washers span
52.27 across the pair, slightly beyond the 52 mm print. Untrimmed 14/16 screws
intersect this cover. Changed washers/hardware require a fresh stack check.
Cable-facing jackposts require 4-40 UNC; prototype rear stems and nuts are M3.
Stem end must remain Y<=6.00; printed passage ends Y6.20. Check flange bearing
and actual stem projection. Do not bend cups or pins to close a flange gap.

GROUNDING AND ASSEMBLY
Provide deliberate J4 bonds to the DB23 shell AND motherboard shell-ground pad.
Do not rely on insulated mounting heads or plastic for continuity.
The right-side wire channel runs from X24 to the edge, Y9.50 +/-0.60, Z0 to1.00.
Pre-tack the motherboard lead with the base removed. It must not lift the base.
Keep wire/solder away from the J3 nut seats and all 23 signal pins.
1. Solder DB23 and PCB together away from print; prepare covered shell joints.
2. Load pins using jig, remove jig, lower joined assembly, seat and solder tops.
3. Fit jackpost nuts/posts; check retention without cover and flange bearing.
4. Mount through original holes, solder tails, finish shell bonds and inspect.
5. Optionally glue cover to tower tops after all electrical and fit checks.

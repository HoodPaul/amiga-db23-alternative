DB23 HOLDER v0.4: SOLID-CRADLE FIT PROTOTYPE

The mating connector is a genuine DB23 solder-cup part. Its assumed rear body
and solder-cup dimensions have NOT been measured. These are actual printable
fit-trial parts. No physical strength, heat tolerance or purchased-part fit has
been verified. PCB artwork remains revision 0.3; the coordinated project and
mechanical assembly are revision 0.4.

REBUILD
From the project root: python3 source/build_mechanical.py
The builder reads source/design_parameters.json, emits editable OpenSCAD,
compiles STL files with native OpenSCAD, checks manifold edges/connectivity,
and exports matching analytic single-solid holder and loading-jig STEP files
with CadQuery. OpenSCAD remains the canonical STL route. CAD tests verify all
23 holder bores, pin/jig clearance, common pin stop and recessed anchor-head
clearances. They do not simulate mating force or establish printed strength.

PRINTED PARTS

1 x DB23_Hole_Fit_Coupon_v0.4.stl, before choosing a pin-guide diameter
1 x DB23_Pin_Height_Jig_v0.4.stl, loading fixture only, not fitted to machine

The holder, loading jig, coupon and individual keeper are each one connected
closed component. The shim pair intentionally contains two separate shims.
The coupon contains the actual 23-hole grid and eight labelled bores from
0.75 to 1.10 mm in 0.05 mm steps. Use actual pins and printer to choose fit.

SOLID CRADLE
The main holder is 52.00 mm wide. Full-height side rails and raised mounting
lugs reach the PCB underside plane at Z5.75. Forward rails start at X+/-18.60
until Y7.10 to clear the assumed connector body and cups. Behind Y7.10 they
start at X+/-17.25 and overlap the substantial 23-pin block. The former rear
window is filled continuously from Y12.50 to15.60 up to Z5.75. These are solid
CAD volumes; select actual slicer walls/infill from physical trials.

The guide block top remains Z4.50, leaving 1.25 mm below the nominal PCB
underside for pin solder joints and heat clearance. Two shallow top recesses
under J3 occupy X+/-18.00 +/-2.20, Y11.80..14.80, with floor at Z4.50.
Their remaining solid floor is 3.90 mm thick. They clear prepared underside
solder fillets, not a soldering tool after seating. Prepare shell leads from
the PCB TOP before lowering the joined PCB/DB23; do not trap wires underneath.
The large rear opening has not been reinstated as an access cutout.

PIN FIT AND HEIGHT LOADING JIG
Assumed square pins are 0.64 mm across flats and 0.9051 mm across corners.
A finished 0.80 mm round bore does not clear them. Nominal guide bores are
0.92 mm, which leaves little theoretical clearance; coupon trials, printer
compensation or an appropriate finishing drill/reamer may require a larger
size. Never force pins into either holder or motherboard.

The default pin length is 10.70 mm: bottom tip Z-3.20, top Z7.50. Actual pin
length, sharp/chamfered tips, motherboard thickness and protrusion must be
measured. The jig has a common flat stop at Z-3.20 and supports the holder
underside at Z0.60, giving 3.80 mm projection below its bottom. Two loose
nominal 2.90 mm pegs, 1.20 mm tall, locate in the printed anchor holes.

Place the EMPTY holder on the jig, then insert all23 precut equal-length pins
until their tips just contact the common stop. A common stop sets equal LOWER
projection; equal UPPER heights also depend on equal pin lengths. Lift the
holder straight off. Confirm the selected pin fit holds every pin at height
without the jig. If pins slide, correct the fit or provide a temporary
heat-safe support; do not assume the printed loading jig is solder-rated.
Remove the jig before applying soldering heat and before loading anchor bolts.

PRINT AND ASSEMBLY COORDINATES (mm)
X is across the port, Y inward from flange front reference, Z above MB top.
Holder STL retains its assembly bottom at Z0.60: drop it onto slicer bed.
Jig STL and STEP are exported in PRINT coordinates with bottom Z0.00; subtract
6.20 mm in Z to show the jig in its loading/assembly position. Its printed
floor top is Z3.00, holder support tops Z6.80, peg tops Z8.00.
Coupon and shims already have bottom Z0. Nut keeper is exported cap-face down,
tongue up. Small horizontal bores and pocket features may need finishing or
local support depending on printer; clear all supports/debris before loading.

Anchor shim centres in assembly: X+/-22.135, Y9.50, Z0.00..0.60. The paired
shim STL has centres X+/-5.00,Y0.00; separate and reposition the two shims.
The single shim is centred at X0,Y0 and only needs translation.
Keeper STL assembly transform: rotate180 degrees aboutY, then translate
X+/-22.135,Y0,Z12.20. Cap Z11.00..12.20, tongue down to Z9.80.

ANCHOR HARDWARE AND FOUR-STAGE ASSEMBLY
Use the original motherboard shell-retention positions, X+/-22.135,Y9.50.
Holder clearance bores are3.30 mm, not an instruction to enlarge the original
nominal3.20 mm motherboard holes. Verify actual fasteners and underside space.
Fit required0.60 mm shims beneath both holder mounting feet before tightening.
The shims preserve the intended height; do not close an unsupported gap.

Raised lugs have LOCAL recessed anchor-head seats at Z2.40. Head pockets are
nominal diameter6.40 mm and open towards the outer edge, deliberately removing
a thin outer lip. Maximum complete head+upper-washer envelope is diameter6.00,
height3.00 mm, so its top is at/below Z5.40, below PCB underside Z5.75.

IMPORTANT: a diameter6 mm head overlaps the PCB outline in plan view. The
anchor bolts must therefore be preloaded AFTER removing the loading jig and
BEFORE lowering the joined PCB/DB23 into the holder. The completed assembly
is then fastened to the motherboard using verified retention from underneath.
Check top driver access, bolt length, underside nuts and chassis clearance.
The head cannot be assumed removable vertically after the PCB is seated.
The outward-open pockets remove a fragile thin outer wall; they do not make
the signal pins or loose locating pegs structural cable-load attachments.

Stage1: solder DB23 to matching PCB, off the printed holder. Prepare top-side
shell leads and underside fillets before the holder hides their underside.
Stage2: use loading jig to set23 pins; remove jig; preload anchor bolts;
lower joined PCB/DB23 vertically onto pins/supports; solder pin tops using
an actual tested heat process. Inspect pin heights and all23 circuits.
Stage3: load captive nuts, insert matching hex jackposts, fit optional caps.
Stage4: fit shims, insert assembly into unpowered motherboard, positively
retain original mounting positions from underneath, solder motherboard tails
and complete shell bonds. Check continuity/shorts before functional testing.

CABLE JACKPOSTS
Cable-facing female threads must be original-compatible4-40UNC. Rear male
stem and captive nut must match. Defaults assume M3 rear stem with5.50 mm AF,
2.40 mm thick nut and0.20 mm total pocket allowance. Do not infer M3.5.
The flange FRONT datum isY0; assumed metalY0..0.50, holder beginsY0.65.
The reference nut pocket spansY1.70..4.30. Jackpost rear stem must end at
Y<=5.50, preferably4.80, to clear the anchor-head region. An8mm stem is not
specified as a drop-in part. Select or trim only after measuring actual parts.

GROUNDING AND LOAD PATH
Cable mating loads need to pass from flange/jackposts through the solid
cradle into positive motherboard/chassis retention. No insertion-force rating
has been established. Verify actual printed material, orientation, walls,
infill, layer adhesion, creep and soldering heat behaviour physically.
Plastic and captured nuts do not provide shell grounding. Provide deliberate
metal/wire bonds to the original motherboard shield-ground points; preserve
the separate SHIELD circuit rather than assuming signal grounds are joined.
